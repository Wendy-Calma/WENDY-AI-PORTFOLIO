What I learned by testing my prompts

Hi, I'm Wendy, a 4th-year BSIT student. This portfolio collects the prompting work I did this semester in PDC10: the prompts I wrote, what the AI gave back, where it went wrong, and what I changed.

How to read this Everything here comes from my own conversations, team projects and class activities. Where something is unfinished or didn't work, I say so.

Featured work

Four projects that show the most about how I prompt, test and fix. The rest are on the Projects and Prompt Engineering pages.

    Jayjay, the J&J Egg Supply chatbot

    A Messenger chatbot for a real egg and rice supplier in Angeles City. It answers in English, Tagalog or Taglish, reads live prices from a Google Sheet, and takes orders that staff confirm. I set up the first version of the code; my teammates Harman and Justine built most of what came after.
