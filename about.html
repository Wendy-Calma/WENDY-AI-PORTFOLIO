About

A student learning by doing

I'm Wendy Rizza V. Calma, a 4th-year BSIT student in section 4A. This portfolio is for PDC60, Conversational AI and Prompt Engineering.

Before this course I mostly typed a question into ChatGPT and took whatever came back. This semester I learned to treat a prompt more like code: write it with a clear goal, test it, find where it breaks, and change one thing at a time.

My teammates I did the class challenges (as team codeX) and the Jayjay chatbot (as team EggDroids) with Harman Magar and Justine Esguerra.

Skills I practiced

  • Testing prompts against written criteria instead of "it looks fine"
  • Revising only the part of a prompt that failed
